This Privacy Policy explains how Hamza ALMALI ("we", "us") handles information when you play Neonbound (the "Game") on iOS and Android. If you have questions, contact us at pronethamza@gmail.com.

1. Summary

  • There is no account system. We do not ask for your name, email address or phone number.
  • Your progress (scores, gems, unlocked cosmetics, settings) is stored only on your device.
  • The Game shows optional rewarded video ads through Google AdMob, which collects some device data as described below. Premium subscribers do not see ads.
  • Purchases are processed by Apple or Google. We never see your payment card details.
  • Leaderboards use Apple Game Center or Google Play Games.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

2. Information stored on your device

The Game saves your game data locally on your device using Unity's PlayerPrefs storage: best scores and waves, weekly local leaderboard, gem balance, owned and selected cosmetics, Premium status, daily-reward timestamps and settings (sound, language, color mode). This data is not sent to us. It is deleted when you uninstall the Game (it may be included in your own device backups, e.g. iCloud or Google backup, which are controlled by you and Apple/Google).

3. Advertising — Google AdMob

Free players can choose to watch rewarded video ads (for example, to continue a run or earn gems). Ads are provided by Google AdMob (Google LLC / Google Ireland Ltd.). To serve and measure ads and prevent fraud, AdMob may collect:

  • device identifiers such as the Apple Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA, only if you allow tracking) or Identifier for Vendors (IDFV), and the Android Advertising ID / App Set ID;
  • IP address and approximate (coarse) location derived from it;
  • device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version) and ad interaction data (impressions, clicks, ad viewing);
  • diagnostic and performance data.

Your choices:

  • Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland and other regions where required): On first launch, a consent message from Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) asks whether you agree to personalized ads and related data processing. You can change your choice at any time from the Game's Settings ("Privacy settings"). If you do not consent, Google may show non-personalized or limited ads.
  • iOS App Tracking Transparency (ATT): On iOS, the Game asks for your permission before your IDFA can be used for tracking. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", the IDFA is not shared and ads are not personalized based on tracking. You can change this in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Android: You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings > Google / Privacy > Ads.
  • Premium: Premium subscribers do not see ads.

Google's use of data is governed by Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy and "How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services": https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

4. Purchases and subscriptions

In-app purchases and the monthly Neonbound Premium subscription are processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play billing systems (integrated through Unity In-App Purchasing). We do not receive or store your payment card or billing address. We receive only the information needed to confirm and deliver your purchase (such as a product ID, transaction ID/receipt and subscription status). Unity Technologies may process limited technical data needed for the purchasing service under its own privacy policy: https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy. Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period; you can manage or cancel them in your Apple ID or Google Play account settings. Refunds are handled by Apple or Google under their policies.

5. Leaderboards — Game Center and Google Play Games

If you are signed in to Apple Game Center (iOS) or Google Play Games (Android), the Game submits your scores to the weekly leaderboard. Your player name/alias, avatar and scores are processed by Apple or Google and may be visible to other players. Your use of these services is governed by Apple's (https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/) and Google's (https://policies.google.com/privacy) privacy policies. You can play without signing in; leaderboard features may then be limited.

6. Crash reports and analytics

We do not use a separate analytics or tracking SDK of our own. If you have agreed in your device settings to share diagnostics with app developers, Apple or Google may provide us with anonymous, aggregated crash reports and usage statistics.

7. How we use information and legal bases

We use the limited data described above only to run the Game, deliver purchases, show leaderboards, and display ads that fund the free version. Where the EU/UK GDPR applies, the legal bases are: performance of a contract (providing the Game and purchases), consent (personalized ads and, where required, storing/accessing information on your device for ads), and legitimate interests (security, fraud prevention, non-personalized ad measurement).

8. Sharing and selling

We do not sell personal information and we do not share it for money. Data is processed only by the service providers named above (Google AdMob, Apple, Google, Unity) for the purposes described. Under some U.S. state laws (e.g. California), allowing an ad network to use identifiers for personalized advertising may be considered "sharing"; you can opt out by declining personalized ads in the consent message or Privacy settings, by choosing "Ask App Not to Track" on iOS, or by deleting your advertising ID on Android. We may disclose information if required by law.

9. Data retention

Game data on your device stays until you uninstall the Game or clear its data. We do not keep server-side copies of your game data. Data held by Google, Apple and Unity is retained according to their own policies.

10. International transfers

Our service providers may process data in countries outside your own, including the United States. They use legal safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses or adequacy decisions.

11. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR, Turkey's KVKK, Brazil's LGPD or California's CCPA/CPRA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or port your personal data, to object to or restrict processing, to withdraw consent at any time, and to complain to your data protection authority. Because we do not hold your game data on our servers, most requests are best handled by uninstalling the Game, using the in-game Privacy settings, or contacting Google/Apple directly. You can always email us at pronethamza@gmail.com and we will respond within the time required by law.

12. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country, e.g. 16 in some EU countries). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the Game, contact us and we will help remove it.

13. Security

Game data is stored locally on your device and protected by your device's security features. Data processed by our providers is protected by their security measures. No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure.

14. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top will change, and important changes will be announced in the Game or on the store listing.

15. Contact

Seçenekleriniz:

  • Android: Reklam kimliğinizi Android Ayarlar > Google / Gizlilik > Reklamlar bölümünden sıfırlayabilir veya silebilirsiniz.
  • Premium: Premium aboneler reklam görmez.

Google'ın verileri kullanımı Google Gizlilik Politikası'na tabidir: https://policies.google.com/privacy ve https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
