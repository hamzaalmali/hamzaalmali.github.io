Fox Media Player Support

Let’s get you playing.

Help for Fox Media Player on Huawei phones and tablets.

Email support

pronethamza65@gmail.com

When contacting us

Include your device model, EMUI / HarmonyOS version, Fox Media Player version and the steps that lead to the issue. Never send your provider password, payment card details or a playlist address containing credentials.

Start here

  1. Check that your internet connection works and your provider account is active.
  2. Confirm the server address and sign-in details with your content provider.
  3. Restart the app and check for updates in HUAWEI AppGallery.
  4. If one channel fails, try another. If every channel fails, contact your provider to check service availability.

Fox Media Player supplies the player only; we cannot activate, replace or restore a third-party service.

Subscriptions & purchases

Purchases are made with your HUAWEI ID through Huawei In-App Purchases. To restore Premium on a new or reinstalled device, sign in to the same HUAWEI ID and use Restore Purchases in the app’s settings.

To manage or cancel your subscription, open Settings → HUAWEI ID → Payments & purchases → Subscriptions on your device, or the Subscriptions page in AppGallery. Refunds are handled by Huawei according to its own policy. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

HMS Core must be installed and up to date for purchases to work; AppGallery installs it automatically on Huawei devices.

Privacy & deleting local data

The privacy policy explains what is stored on your device and which services the app connects to. Remove saved accounts in the app; deleting the app removes everything it stored.
