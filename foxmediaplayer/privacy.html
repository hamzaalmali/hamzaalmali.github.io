Privacy Policy

Fox Media Player · Last updated: November 3, 2026

In short: Fox Media Player keeps your data on your own device. We do not run servers that receive your information, we do not use analytics, advertising or tracking, and we never sell or share your data. The app connects only to the service you sign in to and to the few services listed below.

1. Scope

This policy applies to Fox Media Player ("the app", "we") distributed through HUAWEI AppGallery for Huawei phones and tablets. The app is a media player: it does not provide any channels, movies or series. The service you connect to has its own privacy policy, which we do not control.

2. Information stored on your device

  • Sign-in details for your service: server address, username and password. Passwords are kept in encrypted storage (AES-256, Android Keystore).
  • Preferences: language, playback and subtitle settings, parental control PIN (stored as a hash), hidden or reordered categories and channels, update intervals.
  • Watch progress, favorites and continue-watching items within your own service.
  • Cached catalog and programme guide (EPG) data downloaded from your service, kept to make the app faster.
  • Free trial start date and subscription status, so the app knows whether Premium is active.

We have no copy of any of this information.

4. Optional features

Cross-device sync

If you choose to turn sync on, your favorites, watch progress, hidden items and some settings are copied to the hidden app-data folder of your own Google Drive (drive.appdata permission) after you sign in to Google in your browser. The app cannot see your other Drive files, and nobody else — including us — can see this folder. Watch progress entries may include the stream address provided by your service, which for some services contains your username and password. You can turn sync off at any time and remove the synced copy from your Google account settings.

Notifications

Programme reminders you set are scheduled as local notifications on your device and never leave it.

5. Payments

Premium is purchased through Huawei In-App Purchases. Huawei processes the payment with your HUAWEI ID under the Huawei privacy policy. The app receives only the purchase record needed to confirm that your subscription is active (product, purchase status and expiry). We never see or store your payment details.

6. What we do not do

  • No analytics, advertising, attribution or tracking SDKs.
  • No accounts on our side and no servers that receive your credentials, viewing history or device information.
  • No selling, renting or sharing of personal information.
  • No access to your location, contacts, camera or microphone.

7. Children

Fox Media Player is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from them. Because content comes from the service you connect, parental discretion is advised; the app offers a PIN lock and adult-category filtering.

8. Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays on your device until you remove it. Signing out of an account or deleting a saved account clears its cached content; deleting the app removes everything it stored. Synced copies are removed as described in section 4. Because we do not hold your data, requests to access or erase it under laws such as the GDPR or KVKK are fulfilled by these steps; we are happy to help by email.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date will change when we do, and important changes will be reflected here before they take effect in the app.
